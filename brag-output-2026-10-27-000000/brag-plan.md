# Brag Plan: TallerLab

## What is this app?
TallerLab is a Spanish-language workshop management app for receiving devices, tracking service orders, managing parts inventory, and billing repairs.

## The angle
A repair shop has a lot happening between “received” and “ready for pickup.” Show that whole operation as one calm, legible system: intake, status tracking, parts and labor, then the customer-facing order status. The app’s sober dashboard and blue/teal progress states carry the story.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dark TallerLab sidebar snaps into a clean dashboard. “De recibido a listo para entrega.” The line is framing copy, not a product claim; it leads directly into the actual service status workflow.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Nueva recepción: show device and customer intake form as the starting point.
- Órdenes: a service order moves through “Recibido”, “En diagnóstico”, “En reparación” and “Listo para entrega”.
- A customer status page shows the progress track and the order’s work and parts, then billing closes the loop.

## Outro / punchline
The status track fills to teal at “Listo para entrega.” Close on “TallerLab · Gestión técnica”.

## User flow worth showing
Nueva recepción → update and manage an order (status, work, parts) → customer checks its public status and sees the finished invoice/order details. Use fictional stand-in records only.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, capable workshop operations film
- Interpretation: smooth, precise cuts; restrained typography and motion; let the real interface and status colors carry the energy.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f5f7fa
- Accent: #315cf5 (blue), #16a895 (teal)
- Text: #15233b
- Display font: Manrope
- Body font: DM Sans
- Strongest visual element: dark navy navigation framing the pale dashboard; blue service progress becoming teal at ready for pickup.

## Share copy (draft)
TallerLab keeps the repair shop moving from device intake to “Listo para entrega”—orders, parts, customer status, and billing in one place.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, polished bed with light interface accents.
- Music: bundled “happy beats business moves vol 1” track.
- Music treatment: soft opening, lift across the workflow, gentle fade under the final lockup.
- Music cue guidance: composition-time beat analysis; use only cues that preserve readable scene holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle, restrained glow on the blue/teal status progress.
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched.
- Audio-coupled moments: receipt/intake confirmation, status step updates, final ready state.
- Restraint rule: no loud or playful arcade sounds; keep the product credible.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The shop, in motion — 3s
Dashboard frame from the actual app, with TallerLab brand and “De recibido a listo para entrega.” Show the navy sidebar and real dashboard styling. No real user data; use fictional examples.
Sequential/interaction: Dashboard slides into view.
Audio intent: Warm opening with one restrained UI accent.
Audio-coupled idea: Accent lands on dashboard reveal.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Intake — 4s
Show the Nueva recepción form. Let its actual intake fields and title read clearly; cursor selects a fictional device type and confirms receipt.
Sequential/interaction: Yes — fields focus, then a concise confirmation.
Audio intent: Quiet tactile clicks under the music.
Audio-coupled idea: One soft click on confirmation.
Transition mood: smooth → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Service order — 5s
Show the real order detail/status UI with the status moving through “Recibido”, “En diagnóstico”, “En reparación”. A work/parts row appears to ground the workflow.
Sequential/interaction: Yes — status changes in sequence, with settled holds.
Audio intent: Gentle forward motion, no urgency.
Audio-coupled idea: Low-volume accents on status changes.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Customer update — 4s
Recreate the actual public service-order status page: order heading, device label, progress track, and latest status. Progress reaches “Listo para entrega” in teal. Use fictional order and device details.
Sequential/interaction: Yes — progress steps fill to the ready state.
Audio intent: Small musical lift and a soft confirmation.
Audio-coupled idea: Teal ready-state lands on a music accent if timing allows.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Close the loop — 4s
A clean TallerLab lockup, with compact words grounded in the app navigation: “Órdenes · Inventario · Facturación”. Finish on “Listo para entrega”.
Sequential/interaction: Items reveal with sufficient reading time; then hold.
Audio intent: Warm close and fade.
Audio-coupled idea: Final lockup lands naturally; avoid forcing beat sync.
Transition mood: soft end

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, polished, understated.
**Audio summary:** A warm business bed supports tactile intake and status cues, then resolves on the teal ready state and a quiet brand close.
